Undergraduate thesis, in progress

OfflineNo cloud. No network.

A portable, offline communication aid for people with atypical speech. It runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 with a ReSpeaker XVF3800 mic array and uses few-shot learning to adapt to each dysarthric speaker from a handful of examples.

Occupational safety risk prediction

Group project, PPE monitoring

98.68%helmet accuracy, 93.64% for gloves

YOLOv8 detection plus four classifiers for PPE compliance. MobileNetV3-Small won both tasks at a fraction of ResNet50's size. A SHAP audit caught a risk model that looked 99.27% accurate only because of a leaked feature, and the rebuilt XGBoost model reached 0.9817 ROC-AUC.

The pipeline is grounded in Philippine labor data (RA 11058, PSA ISLE). It showed that gloves, the PPE tied to the most local injuries, were also the hardest to detect.

  • YOLOv8
  • MobileNetV3
  • ResNet50
  • EfficientNet
  • SHAP
  • XGBoost

BEAT-BIT

Group project, wearable TinyML

68 msper classification, 89.3% validation accuracy

A modular heart monitoring wearable. A MAX30102 sensor feeds an ESP32-C3 running an on-device TinyML model trained in Edge Impulse to classify activity states. The challenge was fitting a useful model on a microcontroller while keeping latency low enough to feel instant.

  • ESP32-C3
  • MAX30102
  • Edge Impulse
  • TinyML

HimigTransform

Group project, audio fingerprinting

Shazamrebuilt for noise and for OPM

A noise-robust fingerprinting system for American and OPM songs. It extends Wang's landmark algorithm with SNR-adaptive peak picking and spectral-subtraction denoising, plus a short-query hashing mode for 3 to 5 second clips. It is evaluated on a 100-song database (50 American, 50 OPM) and is the first documented benchmark of Shazam-style fingerprinting on OPM.

  • MATLAB
  • DSP
  • App Designer

Bayad Log

Solo project, web app

1 receiptcounted once, every time

Small merchants paid by GCash screenshot end up with a camera roll of receipts. Bayad Log reads each screenshot with OCR, records it in a ledger, and flags repeated reference numbers so the same payment can't be counted twice.
